How it works

Three calculations behind the result

1

Inflate today's expenses

Your current monthly expenses are compounded by your inflation assumption until retirement.

2

Estimate retirement corpus

The calculator models monthly withdrawals throughout retirement, increasing them with inflation while the remaining corpus earns your assumed post-retirement return.

3

Calculate the funding gap

Your existing savings are projected to retirement. The remaining gap is converted into an estimated monthly investment using your pre-retirement return assumption.

FAQ

Common questions

Why does inflation matter so much?

Inflation reduces purchasing power. A lifestyle costing ₹60,000 per month today can require substantially more rupees decades from now even if the lifestyle itself does not change.

Does the corpus stay invested after retirement?

Yes. The calculation assumes the remaining corpus continues earning the post-retirement return you enter while withdrawals rise with inflation.

Is the monthly investment a guaranteed amount?

No. It is a mathematical estimate based on constant return and inflation assumptions. Actual market returns, taxes, expenses and personal circumstances will differ.
