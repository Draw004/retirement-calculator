Different by design

More than “today's expenses × inflation”

The Carrowmont Retirement Planner separates the pieces that actually drive a retirement plan.

01

Expenses can change

Mark school fees or EMIs as ending, reduce commuting, or increase healthcare and lifestyle costs after retirement.

02

Income offsets withdrawals

Add pension, rent or annuity income so the portfolio only needs to fund the remaining retirement cash flow.

03

Cash flows are modelled over time

The planner evaluates retirement expenses, income and one-time goals across the years rather than relying on one static multiple.
