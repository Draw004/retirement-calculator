Financial disclaimer

An estimate is not a promise.

RetireWise helps you explore scenarios. It cannot know future returns, inflation, tax rules, healthcare needs or how long you will live.

Effective: 17 September 2026 · Educational planning tool

No financial advice

RetireWise provides educational calculations and scenario analysis. It does not provide investment, securities, tax, legal, insurance or accounting advice and does not recommend any security, fund, insurance product, asset allocation or financial institution.

Results depend on assumptions

Every output depends on user-entered assumptions such as inflation, investment returns, retirement age, longevity, expenses and income. Small changes can materially change the estimated corpus and monthly investment.

No guarantee of returns or sufficiency

Actual investment returns are variable and can be negative. Inflation, taxes, healthcare costs, longevity, fees, market sequence and personal circumstances can differ from the model. A displayed funding percentage is not a probability of success and does not guarantee that assets will last.

Taxes and product rules

RetireWise V2.2 does not automatically calculate all taxes, withdrawal rules, fees or legal restrictions associated with EPF, NPS, pensions, mutual funds, annuities or other products. Those rules can change and can depend on individual circumstances.

Your responsibility

Use the tool as one input in a broader planning process. Verify important assumptions and consider advice from appropriately qualified professionals before making consequential financial, tax, legal or insurance decisions.
